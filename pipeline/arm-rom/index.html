Research: the Orbit Joint

A ring-free joint that turns the cuff exactly about the humerus axis. Crank (dark) on the upper-arm bar; coupler (blue) held level by tendon loop 1, so it only translates; cuff bracket (green) on the coupler, turned 1:1 by tendon loop 2. Because the crank vector equals the bracket's offset from the arm axis, the cuff motion is a pure rotation about that axis: 0.0 mm error over ±120°. Three pivots and two tendon loops, nothing encircles the arm, no dead points. On the body it touches the trunk in about 1.5% of poses. It removes the reason every fixed-post shoulder is capped: humeral rotation no longer drags the chain around the shoulder.
