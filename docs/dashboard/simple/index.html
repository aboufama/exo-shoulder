Gap = how far a design is from the targets, lower is better. It adds up the shortfalls in staying 2-14 mm from the skin (target 90 % of the metal), following the arm (95 % of motion frames) and reaching everyday poses (90 %), plus any pose where metal would touch the skin.

Two ways to fail

Each dot is a design. Right = moves with the arm everywhere; up = stays close to the skin. A winner sits in the green corner. Most designs do one or the other.

Names: R = a hinge (revolute joint), P = a slider; "3R" = three hinges in a row (3 degrees of freedom); "spherical" = all hinge axes meet at the centre of the shoulder joint; "offset" = they do not; "3-RRR" = three hinged legs side by side.
