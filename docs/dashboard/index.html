Concept bench record over time

↓ lower is better

The step line is the best gap any morphology has reached so far on the concept bench (every configuration every agent evaluated, unverified until the verifier re-measures it). It turns to the accent colour once it is below the 3R baseline; each new record is a dot on the line and the current one is named. Faint dots are single configurations; a filled diamond is a verifier's re-measured gap.

Built CAD record: R1 over time

↑ higher is better

R1 is the share of the covered skin 2-14 mm from the metal while the exo follows the arm, judged on the built CAD with the exact posed skin (pass at 90%). The step line is the best 3-DOF design so far; each dot is one judged design (filled: full CAD, hollow: keep-out CAD). Values judged before a judging-rule change are faded.

Optimisation over time, per morphology

Lines in the accent colour are below the 3R baseline now; dashed lines are dropped or refuted. The vertical scale is compressed above 30 so that every morphology fits; ticks show real gap values.
