A1How it works

A is the current 3-joint lead with one extra joint at the cuff. Three encoded Ø20 hubs (J1, J2, J3) on MR117-2RS bearing pairs form a spherical linkage centred on the shoulder joint (GH). J1 sits on the acromion top, carried directly on your scapula handler. J1 and J2 point through GH. J3 sits 13.84 mm off GH, near the upper-arm axis, and follows axial rotation.

Link 1 is split by a slaved knuckle, K. A cam on hub 2 (lobe ring → bell crank → dowel nose on a flank) folds link 1 as J2 opens, which keeps the bars close to the skin as the arm rises. K has no encoder and adds no DOF; a clock spring holds it on its stop. At the arm, a flat two-pin knee on MR63ZZ pairs closes the loop to the arm pad, which is machined in one piece with your BOTTOM. A dowel in J3's own ring stops J3 at −111.9° and +122.7°, so the chain never enters poses that would push metal into the skin.

The relay is the 4th joint. A 14 × 14 mm titanium carriage slides 0–35° around a slotted shell strapped to the arm 31 mm above the pad, and a small constant-force coil holds it at zero. Where the chain alone would fold, the carriage turns the cuff side about an axis through GH. That is where A gains its extra reach.

B1How it works

B has three encoded Ø20 hubs on MR117-2RS pairs and three titanium links, with no cam, spring or extra knuckle. J1 sits on the acromion top. J1 and J2 point through the shoulder centre. J3 tilts toward the skin and misses GH by 10.72 mm, near the upper-arm axis.

A flat two-pin knee (pins parallel to J3) joins member 3's strap to the arm pad, which is machined in one piece with your BOTTOM. The knee closes the loop in closed form, so the arm pose sets every link and nothing sags.

Its limit is the J2 fold. The angle between the J1 and J3 axes must stay inside a ~27–109° window, but the full workspace needs 27–141°, so most overhead poses are out of reach.

How it's scored

GapThe shortfall below four targets, in points: reach under 90 %, skin in band under 90 %, follow under 95 %, plus the share of poses with metal in the skin. Score = 100 × (1 − gap / 275).
ReachShare of 360 arm poses the exo follows without folding, jamming or touching the skin.
In-bandShare of the covered skin that sits 2–14 mm from the metal (the aim is 2–10 mm, the 1 cm rule).
Bench / held-outThe bench is 360 arm poses plus 110 motion-clip frames, with the shape frozen from a separate fit. The held-out set is 952 poses and 202 frames never used in design.
Complexity0 means three pinned hubs and three links. Extra joints, loops, springs, sliders and parts add points. A 4th counted joint adds 25.
AuditEvery claim is re-measured by an independent verifier and checked by the alignment auditor. "Watch" means open findings, none blocking.
